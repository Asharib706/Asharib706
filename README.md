## 🧠 About Me

I'm a **Data Scientist & AI Engineer** who bridges classical machine learning and modern LLM-powered systems. My work spans the full spectrum — from training and evaluating ML models to shipping production-grade AI applications including voice agents, RAG pipelines, and multi-agent workflows.

Currently working as an **AI Specialist**, introducing RAG architectures, conversational AI, and agentic workflow automation to enterprise clients.

- 📊 &nbsp; **Data Science:** Statistical modeling, predictive analytics, EDA, feature engineering
- 🤖 &nbsp; **AI Engineering:** LangGraph, LangChain, MCP servers, multi-agent systems
- 🗣️ &nbsp; **Voice AI:** Building real-time voice assistants with ElevenLabs + Gemini + WebSocket
- 🧾 &nbsp; **Document AI:** Production OCR pipelines with vision-language models (Qwen-VL, vLLM)
- 🎓 &nbsp; B.E. Computer Science — **NED University of Engineering & Technology, Karachi**
- 📬 &nbsp; **masharib706@gmail.com**
